I write robotics software in C++ and Python with ROS 2, for robot arms in simulation and on real hardware. At Quantum-Systems I work on the Drone Port, a robotic system that autonomously catches, stores and launches UAVs. My Master's at TUM runs until March 2028.

## Projects

### Desk wiping with a Franka Panda ([franka_panda_ros2](https://github.com/mhayman9/franka_panda_ros2))

A real-time `ros2_control` controller in C++ that combines Cartesian impedance control with normal-force control. The same controller runs in MuJoCo and on a real Franka Emika Panda, and the repository has videos of both. We built it as a team of three in the Robot Dynamics lab course at TUM (Chair of Applied Mechanics).

### Robot kinematics and dynamics in Python ([Robot-Dynamics](https://github.com/mhayman9/Robot-Dynamics))

Our solutions to the exercises of the same course: forward and inverse kinematics, Newton-Euler inverse dynamics, simulation and trajectory planning for an ABB GoFa, a SCHUNK Power Cube and a Franka Emika Panda. The chair provided the course framework, and our team filled in the parts it leaves open.

### Bachelor thesis in autonomous motorsport

At the TUM Professorship of Autonomous Vehicle Systems I developed a method for the safe use of secondary drivable areas in autonomous racing. It covers mapping, a hierarchical decision-making architecture in ROS 2, and safe trajectories for leaving and returning to the racing line, validated in simulation.
